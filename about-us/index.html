ABOUT TRUTH SAB TAK

We look beyond the viral clip.

Truth Sab Tak finds widely shared videos, checks the claims and context around them, and publishes clear analysis to help people understand what they are watching.

What we do

We follow videos gaining attention on public social platforms. Our team investigates what a clip actually shows, what people say it shows, and whether important context has been left out. We publish the analysis as an article so readers can review the evidence themselves.

01 / FIND

Find videos people are sharing

We monitor public conversations and choose viral clips that merit a careful, evidence-based review.

02 / INVESTIGATE

Check claims and context

We look for original uploads, dates, locations, edits and reliable sources that can confirm or challenge a claim.

03 / PUBLISH

Explain what we learn

Each published analysis lays out the claim, evidence, context, sources and a plain-language verdict.

Our standards
We separate confirmed facts from uncertainty, link to supporting sources, avoid political or personal bias, and correct material errors when they are identified. We do not accept video links from visitors for analysis; our editors select videos to investigate and publish.
